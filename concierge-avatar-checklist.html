BRITES JEWELRY · VISUAL ACCEPTANCE

Check the live 3-D guide.

Use a browser with WebGL 2 support. This test page makes no product, cart or store changes.

Open the visual studio

  1. Confirm real rendering. Click Inspect renderer. The result must say mode: "webgl" with a positive frame count. A fallback illustration does not pass this check.
  2. Inspect the finish. Look for smooth ivory and champagne surfaces, aquamarine irises, reflective eye lenses, a sapphire pendant, and a moving shadow on the stage. Check for flicker, missing parts or broken surfaces.
  3. Try every expression. Select idle, listening, thinking, speaking, success and error. Eyes, lids, brows, mouth and posture should respond. These studio controls do not turn on a microphone or speak automatically.
  4. Check pause and resume. Hide the guide, then inspect again: animated should be false. Show it again and confirm animation resumes.
  5. Check accessibility and mobile. With your usual reduced-motion setting, the selected expression should remain visible without continuous animation. At a narrow window size, check that the guide and shopping controls fit comfortably.

If WebGL is unavailable: a clearly identified static illustration is expected. Product search and shopping must remain usable. Visual quality, shadow appearance and frame rate still require a browser that actually renders the 3-D scene.
