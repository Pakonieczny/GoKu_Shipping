Letter charms with hoops: 26 necklace + 26 huggie

Sizes are the cut outline (the library's own size figure adds the drawing's pen width, which was heavier in some source files: the letters themselves are the same).

Each source letter from the charm repo (LETTER_EARRING-0 … 25, a … z) with its two new charms. The letter is copied as it is (same outline); the necklace set keeps its size, the huggie set's letter is made smaller, about 6 mm (a c e n o r s u v x z) to 7 mm (b d f g h i j k l p q t y, m w) on its longest side, as you asked (one already that size is kept). Only the hoop is added, at the top centre (on the stem of b d f h k l t, on the dot of i j), welded into one cut outline, its hole cut.

Necklace ring: Ø 2.3 mm, hole 1.3 mm (as NECKLACE_CHARM-7)Huggie hoop: Ø 3.3 mm, hole 2.1 mm (as NECKLACE_CHARM-7 (HUGGIE))SKUs: LETTER_NECKLACE-n and LETTER_NECKLACE-n (HUGGIE), a = 0 … z = 25 (the shop's twin naming)

To add them to the charm repo (nothing is in the repo until you do this):

  1. Open the Brites Charm Sorter, open the Workspace menu (top right) and choose Master files.
  2. Under ＋ Add a master file, on the line “New charms ready: Letter charms with hoops”, press Add these charms to the repo.
  3. Read the line and press Yes, add them.
  4. Type the repo password in the lock window.
  5. Wait for “Done: 52 charms added to the charm repo”. Pressing again says “Already there”.
