Recent cycles

Every ranked name appears here, not just the ones that fired. A candidate that was stopped is as informative as one that passed — the chip that blocked it is shown first, and the full gate stack is inside.
Exits are evaluated on every cycle. The buy/hold spread holds a position until its cross-sectional rank crosses the median rather than exiting at a fixed threshold — on an identical signal that change moved net returns from +31.5bp/week to +53.1bp/week and cut turnover from ~700% to ~330%. The exit rule is the highest-value use of the 5-minute loop.

Operating mode

Automation stage Research writes candidates and no-trade records but no orders. Each later stage unlocks only after its promotion gate is measured, not asserted.
Dry run — Dry run defaults ON. With it on, the cycle evaluates everything and writes nothing to the ledger.
Cycle interval seconds Netlify documents no minimum cron interval and sub-hourly schedules are known to drift, so nothing assumes an exact fire count.
Kill switch Cancels proposed and unfilled orders and blocks new decisions. Never deletes evidence, history or the ledger.
