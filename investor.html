Astra chooses a 1–3 trading-session limit for each company during initial research, weighing extra upside against risk and locked capital. Targets and stops can exit sooner.

Advanced · historical dates and assumptions

New simulations share paper trading’s decision and execution core. The default is up to three finalists, with cash allowed. The selection is required before starting. Astra returns BUY or PASS for every finalist, with a base allocation of $5,000–$30,000 for BUY and at most $95,000 combined. The shared executor applies strategy multipliers and reduces quantities for cash, concentration, liquidity and loss limits. Baseline purchases use the first available session price; a learned strategy may defer or condition entry, with whole-share rounding, spread and fees. Astra compares one-, two- and three-session horizons, fixes each holding deadline, profit target and stop during initial research, and explains the capital tradeoff. An open position exits at the observed close of its chosen session. A missing or insufficiently liquid deadline bar makes the result unavailable; the holding is never silently extended. No intraday AI research or allocation reruns. Saved older simulations retain their original rules. Each run starts with $100,000 fictitious money. Dates are sampled without repeats. Missing research is built automatically from dated SEC filings and financial statements. Broader historical news and confirmed earnings dates may be unavailable. Company research and daily price history are prepared once in a shared Firebase library. Session prices are downloaded in groups. Simulations read saved data filtered to their own date; they do not download company information separately. Small gaps remain recorded; no prices are invented and no trades execute in missing intervals. Sessions with large gaps cannot run. Prices have the paper feed’s 15-minute delay. Each run follows a common three-session window. Holidays and weekends do not count. Cash released by early exits stays cash; alternative next-day investments are assessed as opportunity cost, not actually traded. Returns exclude dividends and cash interest. Suspected split-like price changes are quarantined; these are exploratory price returns, not total-return accounting. The company range selects research finalists, not mandatory purchases. Risk limits can reduce executable allocations or keep an entire plan in cash. The date range selects starting dates; all three sessions must have ended. Execution assumes a 0.10% spread and $0.005 per share fee, plus the existing stop-slippage rules.

The $1.7635 target allows up to $1.88985 per run, shared across all AI steps without stage-specific holdbacks. Runs stop if the maximum is reached. First-time preparation can take several minutes or longer; saved downloads are reused. Five minutes is a replay target after preparation, not a guaranteed finish. SEC evidence preparation uses no AI tokens; Firebase and data charges are separate.
