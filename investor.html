Interpretation

Current marked book

Nothing here reaches a broker. Approval reserves virtual cash; a separate guard cycle may fill only against a source-hashed bar whose open is later than the decision, feed delay, and execution latency.
A preregistered policy family runs as self-financing shadow portfolios on the same union of eligible opportunities. Each obeys cash, concentration, timing, and cost constraints. None changes in place. Selection uses weighted serially robust inference, DSR/PBO controls, historical stress, and a locked future-paper confirmation; diagnostic weights compare evidence and never split capital.

Operating mode

Measured stage — Manual paper approval can be selected after build attestation. Shadow and limited-auto stages still come only from measured gates.
Operator ceiling Approval enables human-reviewed paper entries. Raising beyond approval only permits the measured ladder to advance after every gate passes.
Operator hold — A hold prevents promotion without erasing evidence or interfering with exits.
Safety identity — Binds account, frozen universe, strategy, variants, fixture result, deployed commit and reconciled paper ledger. Automatic start selects the separately labelled exploratory-paper state.
Dry run — ON means observation-only. OFF enables the simulated paper ledger; this app has no real-money order path.
Cycle interval seconds Netlify documents no minimum cron interval and sub-hourly schedules are known to drift, so nothing assumes an exact fire count.
Entry freeze open A reversible pause on new risk. It blocks proposals, approvals and fills and returns existing entry reservations; exits, marks and stops continue.
Kill switch Cancels proposed and unfilled orders and blocks new decisions. Never deletes evidence, history or the ledger.

Paper learning mode

off
The published settings are the strict comparison policy. On a virtual account, requiring that calibration before collecting outcomes mostly buys silence. This loosens it deliberately so the desk can observe and use its simulated ledger. Every decision still records what the strict settings would have said, so relaxed and strict results remain separable.
Mode Applies from the next cycle.
Act on incomplete information off Gates that block only because data is missing — no earnings window yet, no price history yet, no dossier yet, shares outstanding unknown — abstain instead of blocking. Gates that found an actual problem never relax: a known earnings date, a real fundamental cause, a detected downtrend and broken price data still stop the trade.
Cost hurdle Expected profit must beat modelled trading costs by this multiple. 2 is the published setting and is why nothing fires. 0.5 trades anything with a positive expected edge — those trades will lose money on average after costs, which is the price of the lesson.
Signal strength How far a name must fall below its peers before it counts. 2 standard deviations is the published setting; 1.25 fires far more often and less reliably.
Maximum hold Days before a position is closed regardless. Fractions allowed — 0.25 is about an hour and a half of trading, for fast feedback.
