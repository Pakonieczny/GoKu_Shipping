# Shopping guidance: evidence, behavior and acceptance

Checked 2026-10-08. This document describes the local shopping-guide module and the interaction decisions used to integrate it. Research informs the design; it is not evidence that this implementation improves conversion or has passed hardware usability review.

## Primary research and resulting decisions

| Primary source | Relevant finding or guidance, paraphrased | Implementation decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nielsen Norman Group, [10 Guidelines for Designing Your Site’s AI Chatbots](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ai-chatbots-design-guidelines/), 2026-04-24 | Their participant studies found that page continuity, product context, useful suggested-question buttons, images, and concise expandable information affect whether site chatbots help. | Keep one guide available through the shopping flow. Name the currently viewed product, expose actual next choices as buttons, and retain a small prepared comparison pack while the product remains open. |
| Nielsen Norman Group, [UX Guidelines for Ecommerce Product Pages](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ecommerce-product-pages/), 2019-11-24 | Their ecommerce research recommends complete but concise details, consistent comparison information, visible product options, availability, addition feedback, and a small number of highly relevant related products. | Prepare dimensions and published options before a question is asked; show two related choices initially. Separate same-category alternatives from optional different-category pairings. |
| Amershi et al., [Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/Guidelines-for-Human-AI-Interaction-camera-ready.pdf), CHI 2019 | The evaluated guidelines include appropriate timing, current-task relevance, easy invocation and dismissal, useful explanations, and retaining recent interaction context. | Preparation has no visible side effect. Busy, speech, loading and hidden states suppress an invitation. Respect dismissal, cooldown and previously shown recommendations. Explain every pairing from actual catalogue attributes. |
| Shopify, [productRecommendations](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/storefront/latest/queries/productRecommendations), current documentation | Shopify distinguishes related recommendations from merchant-configured complementary recommendations. The latter are configured through Search & Discovery. | Local content matches are described as suggested pairings, with separate prices. They never imply a merchant bundle, coordinated manufacturing, co-purchase evidence, discount, or official set. A future configured Shopify complementary feed can supply stronger evidence through the same presentation contract. |
| FTC, [Buying Platinum, Gold, and Silver Jewelry](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/buying-platinum-gold-and-silver-jewelry), 2024-11; Canadian Conservation Institute, [Understanding how silver objects tarnish](https://www.canada.ca/en/conservation-institute/services/preventive-conservation/guidelines-collections/metal-objects/understanding-silver-tarnish.html) | The consumer guide explains karat alloys and mechanically applied gold-filled layers. The institute describes sterling as an alloy and silver tarnishing. | Material comparisons may include short, attributed general construction facts alongside each real published value and price. No product-specific base metal, coating thickness, skin-safety guarantee, waterproof claim, or expected lifespan is inferred. |

These decisions are design inferences from the sources. Neither the studies nor this deterministic module establish conversion lift, shopper satisfaction, exact latency, voice quality or rendering quality.

## Local module contract

`brites-concierge-shopping-guide.js` exports `BritesConciergeShoppingGuide.create` in the browser and the same CommonJS factory. Pass checked catalogue products, optionally the shared `BritesCatalogueIntents` vocabulary, the session storage adapter, existing shopper preferences, and a clock for testing.

- `updateProducts(rows)` replaces the local indexed inventory. Duplicate identity conflicts are excluded. Product categories and exact motifs are indexed once; no provider or catalogue request is made by the module.
- `setPreferences(value)` accepts the existing preference shape and direct material, length, theme and item-budget aliases. Preferences remain local; storage contains only dismissed/previously shown handles and suggestion identifiers.
- `prepare(context)` returns the exact current product, verified fact bullets, up to three same-category alternatives, up to three motif-related pairings, literal option suggestions with checked native prices, warnings, and one next-step proposal. Reading a pack never changes a choice or page.
- `suggest({context,message,trigger})` returns a prepared pack and at most one optional invitation; `reply` aliases the invitation’s text for the host. Product opening can offer the next choice; uncertainty can offer alternatives; explicit matching requests can show pairings. Invitations contain at most two products. “What earrings would go with this?” retains the requested earrings category instead of offering a generic charm. “More like that” and “Not those” explicitly request alternatives despite passive cooldown or mute. The host dismisses the literal previously suggested handles before a rejection request, retaining the current listing and selection.
- `markShown(suggestion)` applies the cooldown and repetition guards. `dismiss({kind,handle})` suppresses a class of passive suggestions or one design; `dismiss()` mutes passive guidance for the session. Explicit shopper requests remain answerable. `reset()` starts a fresh guidance session.

An action in a pack is an adapter proposal. The host must verify current product context and capabilities, use its normal current-request execution path, and supply an explicit shopper action before selecting, adding or navigating. Preparation and hover never authorize a mutation. The module does not execute adapter methods, network requests, cart writes, orders or payment.

## Selection and recommendation requirements

Every eligible catalogue row has an owned URL matching the exact handle, a current check within five minutes, complete distinct variants, literal published option groups, known available options, and no applicable identity/cart/recommendation hold. A single available variant must satisfy material, selected options and native-currency item budget together. A cheap silver variant cannot justify recommending a more expensive gold variant. Expiry is checked again while a page stays open.

Explicit material preferences outrank the current selected material. Otherwise the chosen current material is a useful pairing constraint. Gold-filled, plated, solid gold and rose finishes remain distinct. An unlabelled item budget is anchored to the current listing’s currency and disclosed as such; no USD/CAD conversion or combined-set price is invented.

The product-page handle is authoritative over hover history. Bag and checkout pages do not inherit the previous listing. Existing selections restrict compatible choices. When several materials are available, show at most two priced choices and a plain explanation, without preselecting either. Multi-length options require an actual shopper preference or another visible choice. A ready exact selection can propose Add with the actual quantity subtotal. Existing review-ready flows retain their separate visible confirmation.

Related products must have actual name/handle/explicit-tag motif evidence. Description cross-sells do not establish identity. Alternatives use only exact shared motifs within the same category whenever any such candidate meets all stock, material, budget and freshness constraints. Broader theme designs are a clearly explained fallback when no exact motif alternative satisfies those constraints; a strong exact match is never padded to fill a row. Rejecting otherwise eligible exact matches retains the same motif scope. Once those matches are exhausted, an honest no-more response lets the shopper keep the current selection or adjust a preference. Pairings require an exact shared motif and a different category. Items already in the bag and specifically dismissed designs are excluded. Every recommendation carries the actual catalogue check timestamp alongside its exact variant, price and currency. A pairing is a suggestion of separately sold pieces. Historical or cultural meanings continue to use the existing approved knowledge pipeline, never this matcher.

“Anything cheaper?” compares a strictly lower available unit price in the same checked native currency and the actual current published material. An explicit new material preference can change that constraint, with the requested-material qualification retained. Unselected current products use a visibly qualified available from-price. Another product’s cheap silver option cannot justify its expensive gold-filled option. Tax, shipping and a current quantity subtotal never become an invented comparative unit price. A missing price/material reference produces a concise recovery instead of an unsupported cheaper claim.

“Something smaller?” uses comparable published charm/pendant measurements: both width and height must not increase and at least one must decrease, or a shared diameter must decrease. A selected variant’s literal measured size can be compared to the same named option axis. Variable size options require an exact current selection. A “Tiny” title, shorter chain, or one narrow but taller outline does not establish a smaller charm. Hidden script, style, template and comment bodies are removed before measurement extraction. Missing or incomparable dimensions produce an honest size-options recovery. “Cheaper and smaller” retains both constraints on the same actual available variant. `pack.comparison` exposes the qualified reference, requested categories, and `confirmed`, `missing-reference` or `no-match` status.

## Behavioral verification

`tests/growth/concierge-shopping-guide43.test.cjs` covers immediate current-listing details; literal decimal measurements; category-correct alternatives and pairings; hover precedence; bag/checkout continuity; same-variant stock/material/budget conjunction; distinct solid/filled/rose finishes; native-currency and unlabelled-budget handling; inclusive caps and zero-price options; unavailable/unknown/held/parts/service exclusions; expiry/incomplete/future checks; cross-sell and legacy-handle resistance; preferences and exclusions; compatible priced choices; selected-variant mismatches; personalization; direct-add quantity totals; legacy review confirmation; existing adapter action shapes; immutability; no network/mutation side effects; busy/speaking suppression; cooldown; repeat avoidance; session dismissal; malformed identities/options; exact cheaper comparisons; requested matching categories; measured smaller comparisons and missing-size recovery; selected variant dimensions; compound comparisons; and hidden markup resistance.

Browser acceptance must additionally verify real current-page updates, visible menus and suggested-choice buttons, exact selection updates and prices, adding the intended variant only upon a shopper action, cart/checkout continuity, alternate-product navigation and back navigation, and functioning dismissal. Tests with synthetic catalogue rows are behavioral contract checks; they are not evidence of native microphone playback, GPU appearance or live stock accuracy. The host still performs its existing fresh action-time validation.
