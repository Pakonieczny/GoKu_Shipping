# Round 52 independent acceptance and source review

Research checked on 2026-10-10. This document distinguishes source research, authored deterministic acceptance, root-run test evidence and physical/live checks. No test or build is run by this agent: the root owns execution with at most five workers in total.

## Acceptance contract

- Meaning is bound to the exact currently checked piece, the reviewed motif evidence, library version and current shopper topic. Cultural, historical and interpretive claims retain their scope and attribution. A shopper's personal association is clearly separated from a documented interpretation. Missing, stale, held or mismatched meaning cannot borrow another piece's citation.
- Coordinated sets are optional proposals using exact available variants, published motif/category evidence, one currency and stated budget scope. Separately sold item totals are arithmetic, not proof of a bundle, discount, shipping eligibility or tax-inclusive price. Component charms do not silently become necklaces or complete earrings.
- Corrections and exclusions update the next proposal. Declines, hesitation, grief, quoted/private/hypothetical words and provider tool arguments cannot create addition, payment or order authority. Existing individual cart, menu, quantity, private note, engraving, discount staging and consent behavior remains covered by its unchanged acceptance baseline.
- Voice can sound warm and vary cadence while retaining brief truthful replies, interruption cancellation, current-turn/page authority, no replay and no repeat greeting. A prompt or packet inspection does not demonstrate audible naturalness.
- Existing authenticated Firestore is authoritative for durable library, profile, set and conversation records. Bounded browser state supports view/continuity only; it cannot become a second archive, retain credentials/private field payloads, disclose another owner, override a newer correction or resurrect an erased generation.

## Architectural risks to challenge

Library/product versions can drift independently; a reviewed motif record must not be projected to a different product without fresh exact binding. A proposal is not current-page selection or action authority. Overall versus per-item budgets require explicit semantics, especially when the anchor's selected variant costs more than its starting price. Cross-currency prices must not be added or silently converted. A delayed profile/archive response must not overwrite a newer recipient, occasion, motif, material, exclusion or explicit empty topic. Browser queues must acknowledge successful cloud sync without retaining a full duplicate history. Account switching, pending identity, deletion, generation rotation and rejected writes must remain fail-closed. Private setter text and quoted instructions remain data through every guide, model and server fallback.
