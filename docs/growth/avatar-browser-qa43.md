## Evidence limits

The avatar studio's CPU/DOM checks and renderer sample explicitly retain `visualCertification: not_performed`; they cannot replace looking at the rendered face. A held expression sample has no intervening rendered-frame evidence. Its changing authored data cannot prove a visible blink or a full live wait cycle.

If the browser reports only fallback, record fallback visual acceptance and leave WebGL appearance, materials, shadows and FPS open. If WebGL uses software rendering, record that renderer identity and its observed frames; do not present it as hardware GPU performance. Physical microphone input, provider audio quality, word alignment and production commerce are separate checks.
